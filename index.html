Composable Content Architecture — xome.com Redesign

Designed the content architecture behind the xome.com redesign, separating content management from software releases. Pages are assembled from an ordered set of reusable, independently governed sections rather than hand-built templates, so the marketing team composes new pages within brand standards without waiting on an engineering deployment. Around 48 section types share one polymorphic contract, with permitted nesting, layout variants, and required fields enforced by the model rather than by review.

API Surface — Architecture Decision

Argued against a proposed single generic content endpoint and carried the team to separate per-resource endpoints, on the grounds that distinct contracts stay independently testable and confine the blast radius of a future content change to one resource. The decision held, and the shipped API is built that way.
