How I see the world right now

We live in a very good time. Almost anyone, almost anywhere, can do the thing they want to do and are passionate about. That is new, and it is worth saying out loud.

I love Japanese culture. It is exceptional, and I have always admired the focus and the discipline in it. Not everything about it is perfect. I do not like how much they overwork.

I admire China. The growth story is outstanding, built on collective hard work and on the welfare of their people. My one concern is that I am not sure of the intentions behind it.

The United States runs on desire. Desire drives them, and that is not a bad thing — a great deal of what happens in the world happens because of it, and a great deal of the world stays open because of it. My one concern is the goal of dominance, and how far the desire gets pushed.

I single out those three because of what came before. The nuclear bomb was catastrophic in the Second World War. Wars now are not fought physically. They are fought through dominance, and the two main players are China and the United States. China leads in manufacturing, robotics and hardware. The United States leads in AI.

What I do not want is for AI to become the next thing of that kind. The scale is much bigger than the bomb, and the impact could be far more devastating. Can we control the outcome? No. But each of us can make a meaningful contribution, so that the outcome turns out well. That is most of the reason I write anything down.
