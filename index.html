A common engineer

I live in Chennai and work as an engineer at Xome. I talk less and watch more, and I keep things as simple as they will go.

About me

I did not train myself into watching more than I talk. It is how I have always been. I try to reach the reality underneath a problem and then keep the answer as small as it will go. Complication creates confusion. Simplicity gives clarity.

Family comes first, and I mean that about my calendar and not only my feelings. We are four now — my wife, my daughter, and a son who has just arrived. We are an Indian family and we hold to our traditions. I spend less time at work than most people in this line of work and more time at home, on purpose.

I am the most ordinary person you are likely to meet, the neighbour you would not look at twice. Being normal is what makes me special.

What I like

Anime, mostly. Black Clover, Dragon Ball Z and Demon Slayer are the ones I keep coming back to. I have a great deal of respect for Japanese culture, and the part of it I try to carry into my own life is the focus and the discipline — doing one thing properly, for a long time, without needing anyone to watch.

Away from a screen I read science, maths, philosophy, psychology and spirituality.

What I am working on

Clover, an AI Orchestration Framework. It connects real-world Context, human Direction, AI-driven Action and validated Success into a repeatable cycle, and it sits on top of the systems a team already runs.

It is named after the same clover. The framework opens with the common clover — three leaves, the one growing in every field, and how nearly everyone works with AI today. I am a common engineer in that sense. It came out of doing ordinary work and paying attention to it long enough to notice which leaf was missing.
