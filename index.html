Recent work

Clover, an AI Orchestration Framework. It connects real-world Context, human Direction, AI-driven Action and validated Success into a repeatable cycle, and it sits on top of the systems a team already runs.

It is named after the same clover. The framework opens with the common clover — three leaves, the one growing in every field, and how nearly everyone works with AI today. I am a common engineer in that sense. It came out of doing ordinary work and paying attention to it long enough to notice which leaf was missing.

How I see the world right now

We live in a very good time. Almost anyone, almost anywhere, can do the things they want to do and are passionate about. That is new, and it is worth saying out loud.

I love Japanese culture. It is exceptional, and I have always admired the focus and the discipline in it. Not everything about it is perfect. I do not like how much they overwork.

I admire China. The growth story is outstanding, built on collective hard work and on the welfare of their people. My one concern is that I am not sure of their intentions towards other countries.

The United States runs on dreams. The desire to reach the dream is what makes things happen around the world, and it is an open culture, which I admire. My one concern is the idea of dominance, and how far they are going to push for it.

I single out those three because of what came before. The nuclear bomb was a catastrophic mistake of humanity. Wars are not only physical now — they are fought through dominance, and the two main players are China and the United States. China leads in manufacturing, robotics and hardware. The United States leads in AI. I do not want AI to be the next catastrophic mistake. The scale is much bigger than the bomb, and the impact could be far more devastating.

Can we control the outcome? No. But each of us can make a meaningful contribution, so that the outcome turns out well.
