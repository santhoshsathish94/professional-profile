Engineering Principles

Understand before fixing. Investigate the underlying system and establish sufficient context before choosing a solution.

Focus over parallelism. Parallelize only genuinely independent work; coordination itself has a cost.

Prove outcomes, not activity. A successful execution is not evidence of a successful outcome.

Learn from failure and success. Capture validated experience so future engineering decisions become better.

Design for adoption. Technology creates greater impact when teams can understand it, adopt it, and grow with it.
