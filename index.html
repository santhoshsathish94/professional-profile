Engineering Principles

Understand before fixing. Establish enough context to know what is actually wrong before choosing a solution.

Focus over parallelism. Attention is a scarce engineering resource; running more work in parallel is not the same as making more progress.

Prove outcomes, not activity. A successful execution is not evidence of a successful outcome. Validate against the system and state what the evidence actually shows.

Design for adoption. Capability that stays with one person does not change how an organization works.
